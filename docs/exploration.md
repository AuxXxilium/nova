# Exploration

[← Back to the beginner's guide](../README.md)

The galaxy starts dark: **every planet is undiscovered** until a fleet reaches
it. Exploring finds new homes, treasure and danger.

## Charting new worlds

Send any ship (Spy Probes are the cheapest) on a **Spy** mission to an
undiscovered planet. The world is revealed the moment your fleet arrives. When
it does, one of these happens:

- **Just the planet**: a free world you can settle later with a Colony Ship.
- **Another empire's home**: probes along report what they saw.
- **A pirate ambush**: if your fleet survives, the pirates keep the world as a
  *pirate haven* until someone clears it.
- **Derelict ships** drift nearby and join your fleet.
- **A supply cache** fills your holds.
- **An ancient archive** gives you a free research level.

**Spy system** on the Galaxy Map sends probes to every planet worth a look in a
system at once.

> **Tip:** Your star charts are private: only you (and your clan) know the
> worlds you've charted. Other players have to find them themselves.

## Anomalies

Sometimes your explorers pick up an **unidentified signal** at the edge of a
system. Send a fleet to **Investigate** and find out what it is. Bring escorts
and cargo space, because investigating can trigger it.

| Anomaly | What you'll find |
|---|---|
| **Lost fleet** | Drifting ships that join the first fleet to reach them. |
| **Resource field** | Asteroids, a gas cloud or a crystal shoal. Cargo ships harvest it until it runs dry. |
| **Abandoned station** | Stores to salvage, and sometimes ships still docked inside. |
| **Pirate bastion** | An armed pirate base that raids nearby empires. Destroy it to stop the raids and seize its treasury. |
| **Black hole** | A dangerous gamble: a fleet sent in may come back with xenite and new knowledge, be thrown to a distant system, or be lost forever. |

Most anomalies fade after a few days, so don't wait too long.

## Expeditions

The **Expedition** button on the Fleet page sends ships into the deep space
beyond your home system. Choose how long they search; the longer, the better
the chances. An expedition might bring back:

- resources, sometimes even xenite
- lost ships that join your fleet
- a fight with pirates
- a long detour home
- or nothing at all, and now and then a fleet never returns.

**Pathfinders** and **Astrophysics** research improve your chances, and
Astrophysics lets you send more expeditions at once. Expeditions can bring an
illness home too.

## Seeing the galaxy

- A **Deep Space Scanner** watches the space around its planet: foreign fleets
  flying there show up on your **Radar**, and you can intercept them. A bigger
  scanner sees farther.
- Scanners also hide your own fleets: another empire's scanner only sees your
  fleets if it is better than your best scanner. Fleets of pirates and of
  empires without any scanner are always seen.
- Scanner ranges are drawn as circles on the Galaxy Map, and fleets the
  scanners see move across it as markers.

## Moons and space stations

- **Moons** can form from the wreckage of really big battles, and some systems
  have one from the start: free, held by pirates, or by an AI empire.
- **Space stations** in some systems are held by pirates, with no ships but
  heavy defenses and a hoard of treasure. Beat them, take the hoard, and settle
  the station with a Colony Ship.

See [Your planets](planets.md#moons-and-space-stations) for what they're good
for.

# Research

[← Back to the beginner's guide](../README.md)

Research belongs to your whole empire: what you discover counts on every
planet you own, and on every planet you'll ever own. You need a **Research
Lab** to start.

## How research works

- One research runs at a time, and you can line up more behind it. The
  **Parallel Works** buff from the Xenite Exchange runs several at once.
- At the top of the Research page you choose how fast it goes:
  - **All labs** adds up the Research Labs on all your planets, so even a
    colony without a lab can start research.
  - **This planet** uses only the lab on the planet you're looking at.
- The cost is paid from the planet you start it on.
- Higher levels need a better Research Lab and higher levels of related
  research. A locked card tells you exactly what's missing.

## Energy and engineering

| Research | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Energy Technology** | Better power from Solar Arrays and Fusion Reactors, and the base for most other research. A good first choice. |
| **Planetary Engineering** | Lets you develop your planets further: each level allows one more step of Planetary Development. |
| **Xenotechnology** | Unlocks xenite mining and the exotic ships and defenses built with xenite. |

## Weapons and protection

| Research | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Laser Technology** | Light weapons. Needed for laser cannons and many ships. |
| **Ion Technology** | Charged particle beams, for ion cannons and cruisers. |
| **Plasma Technology** | The strongest classic weapons. It also helps your metal and crystal mines a little. |
| **Weapons Systems** | Makes all your ships and defenses hit harder. |
| **Shielding Technology** | Stronger shields for ships, defenses and the Planetary Shield. |
| **Armor Plating** | Tougher hulls for ships and defenses. |
| **Ship Weapon Calibration** | Extra firepower for ships only. |
| **Ship Shield Harmonics** | Extra shields for ships only. |
| **Defense Targeting Grid** | Extra firepower for defenses only. |
| **Fortress Shielding** | Extra shields for defenses and the Planetary Shield dome. |

Research bonuses from different lines stack on top of each other, so a
specialist line adds to what Weapons Systems or Shielding already gives.

## Drives and travel

| Research | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Combustion Drive** | Faster small ships. Needed for Spy Probes, your first explorers. |
| **Impulse Drive** | Faster medium ships, and unlocks several of them. |
| **Hyperspace Theory** | The science of folded space, needed for big ships and the top levels of the Shipyard. |
| **Hyperspace Drive** | Faster heavy ships, and unlocks the biggest hulls. |
| **Gravitic Navigation** | Makes every ship a little faster. |
| **Hold Compression** | More cargo space in every ship: more loot, bigger transports, fuller harvests. |
| **Fuel Recycling** | Your fleets burn less fuel. |
| **Quantum Drive** | A one-time breakthrough far in the future: unlocks **Quantum** versions of every ship, twice as fast and a little stronger. |

## Exploration and spying

| Research | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Fleet Computers** | Lets you have more fleets in flight at the same time. |
| **Espionage Technology** | Better spy reports, and better protection against other people's spies. The higher you are above your target, the more your probes see. |
| **Astrophysics** | Better star charts: explorers and expeditions find more, and you can send more expeditions at once. |

## Where to start

1. **Energy Technology**: better power, and needed for almost everything.
2. **Combustion Drive**: your first explorers, the Spy Probes.
3. **Laser Technology** and **Impulse Drive**: your first real defenses and
   warships.
4. **Fleet Computers**: more fleets at once, once you explore a lot.
5. **Espionage Technology** before you start attacking anyone.

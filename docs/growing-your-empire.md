# Growing your empire

[← Back to the beginner's guide](../README.md)

## Player level

Everything you build, research and launch makes your empire stronger, and
your **player level** grows with it. Your account menu (your initials, top
right) shows your level and how far the next one is. A level once reached is
kept, even if you lose ships later.

Some things wait for a level:

- The **Xenite Dreadnought** can only be built from a high player level.
- The **last, strongest level** of many buildings unlocks at an even higher
  one.

## Rewards

The **gift button** in the top bar opens your rewards:

- **Welcome rewards.** One a day you play during your first two weeks, seven in
  all: resources, ships, xenite and a production boost. They open by themselves
  while today's is waiting.
- **Level rewards.** Every fifth player level brings resources and xenite, and
  some bring a boost for a week. Rewards wait until you collect them, so
  **Collect all** picks up everything at once.

Rewards are always delivered to your capital.

## The Xenite Exchange

The shop button next to your messages opens the **Xenite Exchange**.

**Trade xenite down.** Turn xenite into metal, crystal or deuterium on the
planet you're looking at. Nothing turns resources back into xenite, so spend
it wisely.

**Buy buffs.** Spend xenite on boosts for your whole empire, for a week or a
month:

| Buff | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Rich Veins** | Every mine produces more. |
| **Fuel Efficiency** | Fleets burn less fuel, and docked warships use less deuterium. |
| **Overdrive** | Faster fleets. |
| **Battle Doctrine** | Stronger ships and defenses. |
| **Rapid Logistics** | Faster building, research and shipyard. |
| **Master Builders** | Lower prices for everything you build and research, and more of your defenses rebuilt after a battle. |
| **Parallel Works** | Several orders at once in every queue. |
| **Living Space** | More people on every planet. |

Prices in the game always show what you really pay with your active buffs.
Buying a buff that's still running adds the time to it.

## Clans

On the **Clan** page you can found a clan or accept an invitation.

- **Shared star charts.** Worlds and anomalies one member charts are known to
  the whole clan.
- **No friendly fire.** Members can't attack or spy on each other.
- **Defend each other.** Send ships to guard a member's planet; they fight
  beside its defenses against every attack.
- **Attack together.** Join a member's attack on its way, and arrive together.
- **Clan board.** Talk, and ask for resources. A request has buttons to send
  what's asked right away.
- **Member perk.** The leader picks a free buff for every member. It gets
  stronger the more members are active.
- **Clan buffs.** Any member can buy a buff from the Exchange for the whole
  clan.
- **Alerts.** When someone attacks a member, the rest of the clan is told.

## Rankings and messages

- **Rankings** list every commander and AI empire by points, and by economy,
  research, military, planets and discoveries.
- **Messages** keep your mail, battle reports, spy reports and alerts. Write to
  any commander from Messages, the Rankings or their planets on the map.

## Your settings follow you

Your theme (light or dark), accent colour, the layout of your Overview cards,
your sorting and filters are saved with your account. Log in from another
computer and everything looks the same. Phones keep their own settings, so
your phone can look different from your desktop. A new phone starts with your
desktop's settings.

# Fleets and combat

[← Back to the beginner's guide](../README.md)

Everything that leaves your planets flies as a **fleet**: explorers, freighters
and warships alike. This page explains the missions, how to attack and
intercept, and how battles are fought.

## Sending a fleet

Pick a target on the Galaxy Map (or right-click a planet) and choose a mission.
The **fleet dispatcher** opens in three steps you can switch between freely:

1. **Target & mission**: where to, and what to do there.
2. **Ships**: which ships go. A card for the mission can pick them for you:
   enough cargo ships for your cargo, enough Recyclers for a debris field,
   enough probes for a full spy report, or a fleet likely to win an attack.
3. **Cargo & options**: what to load, and when to leave.

Flight time, fuel and arrival are always shown before you launch.

**Good to know:**

- **Fuel travels in the hold.** The deuterium for the trip takes cargo space,
  and on a round trip half of it is kept for the way home. A fleet whose hold
  can't take its fuel can't launch, so add cargo ships.
- **Crews need supplies.** Every fleet carries water and food for its crews.
  Whatever is left comes back home.
- **Fleet slots.** You can only have so many fleets out at once. Fleet
  Computers research raises the limit.
- **Recall** a fleet any time on its way out: it turns around and flies home.
- **Timed launch.** Instead of now, a fleet can **Launch at** or **Arrive at** a
  time you choose, even while you're offline.
- **Repeat.** A transport or recycling run can repeat by itself until there's
  nothing left to do.
- **Waypoints.** A fleet can fly by up to three places on its way, for example
  to avoid enemy scanners. Detours cost time and fuel.

## Missions

| Mission | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Spy** | Chart an unknown world, or send Spy Probes to see what another planet holds. |
| **Transport** | Deliver cargo to a planet and fly home. |
| **Deploy** | Move ships and cargo to one of your planets for good. |
| **Colonize** | Found a colony on a free world with a Colony Ship. |
| **Attack** | Fight a planet's ships and defenses, and plunder it if you win. |
| **Invade** | Attack an enemy colony and land troops with a Colony Ship to take it over. |
| **Recycle** | Collect a debris field with Recyclers. |
| **Intercept** | Catch a foreign fleet in deep space and fight it there. |
| **Defend** | Guard a clan member's planet for a while; your ships fight beside theirs. |
| **Park in orbit** | Wait at a planet for a while, then come home. |
| **Trade** | Offer your cargo to another player for a price they can accept or decline. |
| **Investigate** | Explore an anomaly: find out what a signal is, harvest a field, salvage a station. |
| **Expedition** | Search deep space beyond your home system. Started from the Expedition button on the Fleet page. |

## Attacking a planet

1. **Spy first.** Send Spy Probes to the target. The report shows its
   resources, ships, defenses and buildings. Without a report, you're
   attacking blind. A planet with a Deep Space Scanner needs many probes for a
   full report, and fewer probes only show part of it.
2. **Check the forecast.** When you choose Attack, the dispatcher plays the
   battle out many times against what you know and shows your chance to win
   and the expected losses on both sides.
3. **Let the game suggest a fleet.** On the Ships step, the attack card can
   pick the smallest fleet likely to win while losing no more than you allow.
   You can leave ship types out.
4. **Bring cargo space.** If you win, your fleet plunders part of the planet's
   resources, as much as your holds can carry.
5. **Bring Recyclers**, or send them right after: every battle leaves a debris
   field of wreckage that Recyclers turn back into metal and crystal. Use
   **Arrive at** (or **After your attack**) to time them.

**Attacking together.** Clan members can join each other's attacks if their
ships can get there in time: all fleets arrive at the same moment and fight as
one.

**Protection.** New players are protected until they've grown a little. Nobody
can attack them, and they can't attack anyone either.

## Invading a colony

Bring a **Colony Ship** along and choose **Invade**. The fleet fights like an
attack. If you win and nothing on the planet fights back (every ship and
defense gone, and the shield dome down), your troops land and the planet is
yours.

- A conquered planet loses many of its people, and its buildings lose some
  levels.
- Home worlds of players can't be invaded, only colonies.
- You need a free colony slot, and after a conquest your forces need a rest
  before the next one.

## Intercepting a fleet

You can catch other fleets in deep space before they reach their target.

1. **See them.** A **Deep Space Scanner** shows foreign fleets flying near your
   planets. They appear on the **Radar** tab of the Fleet page, as a yellow card
   on the Overview, and as moving markers on the Galaxy Map.
2. **Press Intercept** on the fleet (on the radar, the Overview card or its map
   marker). Your fleet flies to where the target will be and fights it there.
3. **Be quick.** You have to reach it before it lands. The dispatcher tells you
   whether you can.

**What you win.** Destroy the fleet and you take part of its cargo, plus some
of the material of the ships you destroyed, as far as your holds have room.
No debris field forms in deep space.

**Hunting together.** Fleets that reach the same target close together fight as
one, whoever sent them, and share the spoils.

**They can escape.** The owner is warned when you send interceptors. If they
turn back in time, your hunters find nothing and fly home.

> **Tip:** Fast ships such as Interceptors are the best hunters. Fleets parked in
> orbit at your planets are easy targets, because they don't move.

## How a battle works

- A battle lasts a few rounds. In each round, every ship and defense fires at a
  random enemy.
- Shields take the damage first, then the hull. A unit is only destroyed when
  its hull is gone. Very weak shots bounce off strong shields.
- Big ships with several guns can take out several small ones in one round.
- Cargo ships, probes and other civil ships are only shot at once their side
  has no warships or defenses left.
- Ships that survive keep their damage until it's repaired.
- **Battle reports** play out round by round, so the outcome stays exciting.
  Click a round to see what each side lost in it. Every report has a
  **Rebuild** button to queue what you lost.

## Debris and recycling

Every battle leaves a **debris field** of metal and crystal from the destroyed
ships. Send **Recyclers** to collect it. Cargo ships flying with them add
carrying space. A field drifts apart after a while, so don't wait too long. If
ships guard the planet, your recyclers have to win a battle first.

A really big debris field can sometimes pull together into a **moon**.
